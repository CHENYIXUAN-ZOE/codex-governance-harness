# Assured controls

Before the consequential action:

- identify affected people, systems, data, and irreversible consequences;
- check that existing authorization covers the action; do not ask again when target, scope, and consequences are already clear;
- choose the narrowest permission and smallest blast radius;
- define preconditions, rollback or recovery, and stop conditions;
- protect credentials and sensitive data;
- prefer a dry-run, staging environment, backup, snapshot, or canary.

Before completion:

- verify the intended outcome and important negative conditions;
- review the final diff or external state;
- obtain independent review when consequences justify it;
- record residual risk and recovery instructions in the project's chosen authority.

Pause only the dependent action when a missing choice would materially change consequences or authority. Complete independent authorized preparation so any necessary approval concerns a concrete, reviewable result.
